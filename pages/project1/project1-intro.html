project 1 | the website

tech trends

Original web development project

I built Tech Trends Blog as an early hands-on project to learn how a content-focused website should be organized. I worked on the layout, responsive navigation, article structure, and performance rather than starting from a ready-made template.

The site gave me a chance to apply HTML, CSS, and JavaScript alongside design planning in Figma and Relume. I used ChatGPT to help draft content and Midjourney and Canva to develop visuals; I made the layout and implementation decisions myself.

One of the harder parts was balancing graphics and effects with readable pages and practical load times. I tested different approaches and revised the design as I learned what worked.

I deployed the project on Netlify. It remains a record of my early development work, including how I moved from a design idea to a functioning website.
