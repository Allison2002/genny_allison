Original web development project

During development, Sports Tube Community recorded 100 in PageSpeed Insights performance tests on mobile and desktop. I used those tests to check image delivery and front-end changes. The numbers describe that test period, not a permanent score or an accessibility certification.

(These are historical PageSpeed screenshots. Current scores may differ by test date, page, and device.)

  • AI-generated content and visuals: Ensuring the highest quality while maintaining fast load times.
  • Precision image optimization: Striking a balance between visual appeal and performance.
  • Streamlined code: Eliminating inefficiencies to enhance functionality and reduce load times.
  • SEO-focused content structure: Improving readability and discoverability.
  • Advanced Core Web Vitals optimizations: Reducing render-blocking resources and perfecting load times.

I used AI tools for some draft content and imagery, but the important front-end work was testing the layout, adjusting assets, and keeping pages responsive.

The project gave me practical experience comparing before-and-after results instead of assuming a change was an improvement.
