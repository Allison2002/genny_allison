01 / THE STORYARCHITECTURE · INVESTIGATION · AI WORKFLOWS

HOW I THINK

I like figuring out how things work.
And why they don't.

I'm a technical problem-solver drawn to the space between information architecture, complex investigations, and AI-assisted workflows. I enjoy taking a complicated system, understanding its moving parts, and finding a clearer way forward.

My path into technology wasn't traditional. Before web development, I worked in business, finance, and operations and ran my own business. Those experiences taught me to take ownership, ask questions, and make practical decisions.

I taught myself web development and built this portfolio by hand using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. That foundation grew into real-world work with WordPress, website architecture, technical quality checks, and AI-supported investigation systems.

Today I work on complex digital platforms, where the challenge is often not just finding a problem—but understanding the evidence, dependencies, and risks before recommending a fix.
